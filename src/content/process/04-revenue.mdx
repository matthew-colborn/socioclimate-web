  Aim the record and what it shows at the activities that actually generate

  Marketing, pricing and follow-up that run off what the business knows about

This is the move the other three exist for, and it is where a data project stops
being a data project.

A business that can see which jobs were worth taking can price the next ones
differently. One that knows which enquiries turned into work can spend its
marketing budget on the sources that produced them instead of spreading it
evenly out of ignorance. One that holds two years of its own demand signal knows
things about its own market that cannot be bought, because nobody else was
standing where it was standing.

We run this rather than advising on it. The marketing, the follow-up, the
pricing decisions and the capital questions around them are part of the work,
because a recommendation handed to a business that is already at capacity does
not get executed, and an operating system nobody points at revenue is just
tidier software.
