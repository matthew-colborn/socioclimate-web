name: Understand it
line: >-
  Turn the raw record into something legible, so the business can see what is
  happening and decide from it.
result: >-
  A small number of views that answer the questions the owner actually asks,
  rather than a dashboard nobody opens twice.
---

A record nobody can read is a cost. This move is the difference between having
the data and having the answer.

The work is mostly subtraction. Every business has three or four questions that
actually govern how it spends money and time — which work is worth taking, which
customers are worth keeping, where the next month's revenue is coming from, what
is quietly losing money. Those get answered directly, in the terms the owner
already uses. Everything else is left out, because a screen with forty numbers
on it communicates nothing and gets ignored by the second week.

The test is not whether the reporting is comprehensive. It is whether somebody
changed what they were going to do because of it.
