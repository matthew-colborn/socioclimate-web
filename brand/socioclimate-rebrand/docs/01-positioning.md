# 01 · Positioning

The live socioclimate.io calls SocioClimate a data-leverage company for service businesses ("Dormant data in, decisions and revenue out"). That framing moves to the Services page, where it is still true. The company line changes. The mission line, "Building and compounding data moats," carries over.

## The line

**SocioClimate builds the system of record for fragmented markets.**

"System of record" is a term of art in vertical software. It tells a tech buyer and an allocator the same thing: we own the authoritative record of a market, and that record is where the value sits.

Do not call SocioClimate "a real estate technology company." That caps the company at one sector. Use a sector qualifier only when the audience is real estate ("our first two records are in real estate"), never as the identity.

## Supporting lines

| Use | Line |
|---|---|
| Mission (internal and About) | Building and compounding data moats. |
| One sentence | We find markets where the facts are scattered, build the record, and keep it current. |
| The loop | Services fund the databases. The databases make a record nobody else has. The record is the research edge. |
| The method | Find the gap. Build the record. Wire the sources. Keep it current. |
| Honesty line (site footer of Databases) | Every field carries a source and a date. Stale data is flagged, not hidden. |

## Structure: one company, three arms, one loop

1. **Services** (funding arm). Website design and back end integration for operators: AppFolio, Follow Up Boss, IDX. Small roster by design, with a stated ceiling and a waitlist.
2. **Databases** (product line, the center). Each database is an *instance* of the same method. Instance 01: the Deed-Restricted DB (deedrestricted.org). Instance 02: the building power record. Future instances run the same four steps on a different fragmented sector: industrial power, marine slips, aviation hangars, or outside real estate entirely.
3. **Research** (investment arm, research stage). Working papers, operating papers, and a public REIT prediction log. It starts as writing and a scored public record before it ever manages capital.

## What the site must prove

The databases are used by someone other than us. Until deedrestricted.org is on a public URL with outside traffic, its stage on the site stays "Building," as it reads today, not "Live." The site never claims more than is true. That honesty is the brand.

## Audiences, in order

1. Operators who might hire Services (property managers, brokerages, service businesses).
2. Data and proptech peers, potential acquirers or partners for a database.
3. Allocators and researchers reading the Research arm. They are readers, not prospects. No solicitation (see 06-voice-and-compliance.md).
