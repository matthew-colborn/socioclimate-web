Stage labels here are accurate rather than flattering. One is live, one is in
staging, one is being built, and one is an operating partnership. Where a real
figure exists it is on the page; where it does not, the space is left out rather
than filled with something that sounds like one.

One of these is ours rather than a client's. It is included because it is the
same argument with the client removed — a market whose valuable data nobody was
keeping, and what it looks like to start keeping it.
