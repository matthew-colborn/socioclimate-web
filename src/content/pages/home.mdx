## What we are

A data-leverage company. We partner with existing service businesses and turn
the valuable data already inside them into better decisions and more revenue.

The delivery mechanism is custom software. The product is what happens to the
business: data that was dormant and invisible becomes observable, drives
decisions, and earns.

We are not a dev shop and not a software vendor. A dev shop builds what you
specify and hands it over. We find the leverage hiding in your own data and
build the system that turns it into money — which means we have to understand
the business, not just the brief.

## Why it holds together

The operating work and the research behind it are the same idea.

The published research argues that observability — making an asset's data
frequent, standardized and visible — is what makes pricing and automation
possible, and that value follows. SocioClimate applies that argument to
operating businesses: take one whose valuable data is unobservable, make it
observable, and the decisions and the revenue follow.

The research demonstrates it in markets. The partnerships apply it in
businesses. One engine, two arenas — which is why the portfolio includes a
market we built the record for ourselves alongside the companies we built it
with.
