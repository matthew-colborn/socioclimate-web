This is the fullest case, and the one that shows what we actually are. With
<Client /> the work was not a handover — we built the systems, ran the
marketing, and sat in the decisions about financing and direction.

**What they own now.** Every job scheduled through the operations system is a
record: who wanted what, where, how often, at what price, and what happened
afterwards. That accumulates in their system, under their name, as a by-product
of the crews doing their work. A cleaning company that has been running two
years on a system like this knows things about demand in its own market that
nobody else can look up.

In an ordinary build that record would be sitting in a scheduling vendor's
database, and it would leave with the vendor.
