# 06 · Voice and compliance

## Voice

Grounded, precise, quietly confident. Operator grade: someone who builds real systems and knows the business they serve. One claim per section, stated plainly, then the evidence.

- No em or en dashes in copy. Use periods and commas.
- No bold inside sentences.
- No invented statistics, clients, logos, testimonials or quotes. If it is not in `04-copy-deck.md` or `content/*.json`, it does not ship.
- Name things by what the reader recognizes (AppFolio, income limits, jurisdictions), not by how the system is built.
- Short sentences. Active voice. No hype vocabulary.

## Compliance routing (do not resolve these in copy)

Anything in these categories gets a `[VERIFY with Latoya]` tag in the copy, a placeholder that is safe by default, and a line in the PR description. Matthew routes them to Latoya before launch.

1. **Securities language.** The Research arm and the word "investment" anywhere. Default: no performance figures, no "returns", no "fund", no "capital raise", no "invest with us", no LP or accredited-investor language, no contact topic called "Investing". The disclosure in the footer and on every research page is required.
2. **DRE advertising.** Any mention of brokerage services, tenant representation, listings or buyer representation. If kept, the page needs the licensee name and DRE number and the broker identification required by policy. Default: describe the database's use without offering brokerage services on this site.
3. **Fair housing.** deedrestricted.org copy and the SC-01 page describe programs and eligibility rules. Never describe who a program is "for" in terms of protected characteristics. Use program terms (income limits, household size, owner-occupancy).
4. **Privacy.** The contact form needs a privacy notice link. Do not add analytics that set third-party cookies without a consent banner; prefer cookieless analytics.
