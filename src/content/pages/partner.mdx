<Col kicker="Door one" title="Partner with us" cta="Tell us what you already record" mail="partner">

For operators who already generate the raw material. Every day your business
produces records that exist nowhere else and that nobody has normalized — the
by-product of doing the work. You are sitting on the first layer of a data asset
and have no particular reason to be good at building the rest of it.

### What we bring

- **Schema design.** Deciding what the record is, before it accumulates in a
  shape that cannot be queried later.
- **Normalization.** The thirty small interpretation problems. This is the part
  that is tedious rather than hard, and it is the part that compounds.
- **Productization.** Turning a normalized record into something with a price on
  it, a delivery mechanism, and a reason to renew.
- **The buyers.** Knowing who pays for the answer, and what form they need it in.

### What we look for

The same four conditions as anything else we build:

1. A recurring stream of records nobody else sees.
2. Governing rules that are public but scattered across jurisdictions.
3. A segment too small for a national vendor to bother with.
4. An administrative rather than market-set price.

You keep operating. Nothing about the day job changes.

</Col>

<Col kicker="Door two" title="Sell us your company" cta="Start a conversation" mail="acquire">

For owners of data or services businesses. Being plain about what you would be
dealing with matters more here than a pitch does.

SocioClimate is a small operator, not a fund. There is no blind pool behind
this. There is no auction process, no forty-eight-hour letter of intent used as
a negotiating device, and no associate who will hand the file to someone else
after signing. You would talk to the person who will still be running the
business afterwards.

### What we buy

Businesses that produce a record as a by-product of operating — data, services,
or software with a data exhaust — where the record is worth more than the
current owner has been able to make it worth.

### What happens after

We hold. The reason a small operator can pay a fair price for a business like
this is that the record keeps accruing after the sale, and a fund with a horizon
cannot underwrite that.

</Col>
