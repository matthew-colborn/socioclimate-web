---
title: Data moat diagnostic
description: Five questions on whether your business has a data moat.
heading: Do you have a moat?
standfirst: >-
  Five questions. The answer appears as you go, and nothing is held back behind
  an email address.
questions:
  - key: stream
    text: Does your business produce a recurring stream of records nobody else sees?
    help: >-
      Not a dataset you bought or a report you can order. Records that come out
      of doing the work, that exist because you did it.
  - key: scattered
    text: Are the rules governing your market public, but scattered?
    help: >-
      Published somewhere by someone, a regulator, a council or an association,
      but spread across jurisdictions, formats and PDFs that nobody has pulled
      together.
  - key: administrative
    text: Is the price set administratively rather than by open bidding?
    help: >-
      A formula, a schedule, a committee, or a rate card. As opposed to a market
      where buyers and sellers publish what they paid.
  - key: tedious
    text: Is the work of assembling the record tedious rather than technically hard?
    help: >-
      Many small interpretation problems rather than one large engineering
      problem. The shape a national vendor never gets round to.
  - key: history
    text: Are you keeping the history, not just the current state?
    help: >-
      The sequence, not the snapshot: what it was last year and the year before.
      Every other component can be acquired late. This one cannot be bought back
      at any price once the window has passed.
