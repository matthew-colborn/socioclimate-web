The public site is the visible half. The half that matters is underneath it —
the search surface people actually use, and the capture and follow-up systems
that decide whether an enquiry becomes a conversation or disappears.

Every enquiry that arrives is also a record: what people searched for, what they
asked about, what they did next. That accumulates whether or not anyone is
paying attention to it, which is the argument for paying attention to it.
