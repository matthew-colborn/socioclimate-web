name: Find the data
line: >-
  Locate the valuable data already sitting inside the business — usually dormant,
  usually invisible, almost never missing.
result: >-
  A plain account of what the business already knows that nobody outside it can
  see, and which parts of it are worth building on.

Almost every service business is already producing the record. Jobs booked and
what they were worth. Which customers came back and which did not. What was
quoted against what was accepted. Who asked for what, in which part of town, in
which month.

The reason it is not doing anything is rarely that it was never collected. It is
that the record is scattered across a scheduling tool, an inbox, a spreadsheet
and somebody's memory, in shapes that cannot be compared to each other. Data in
four formats is not a smaller version of data in one — it is data you cannot ask
a question of.

So this move is an audit rather than a build. What exists, where it sits, what
condition it is in, and which of it would change a decision if anyone could see
it. Most of what turns up is discarded. What survives is the short list of
things that are both genuinely held only by this business and genuinely capable
of moving money.
