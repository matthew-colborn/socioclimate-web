and is not yet in front of customers.

**What they own as a result.** Every transaction moved through it leaves a
record of what happened, when, at what price, and who did what — held in the
brokerage's own system rather than scattered across a national platform's. That
is the same argument as everywhere else on this page: the software is the thing
you notice, and the record is the thing that compounds.
