This is the entry that answers the obvious question about the rest of them.
Building a data asset is one thing. Running alongside an operating business
while it happens is another, and it is the part that decides whether the record
is any good.

With <Client />, the work was not a handover. We built the website and the
client operations management system, ran the marketing that fills it, and
assisted with financing and overall strategy. The operations system is the piece
that matters here — every job scheduled through it is a record of who wanted
what, where, and how often, produced as a by-product of the crews doing their
work.

That is the same shape as everything else on this page, arrived at from the
opposite direction: not a data project that needed an operator, but an operator
that turned out to be producing data.
