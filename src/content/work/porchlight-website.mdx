Moves one and four on a single surface. The valuable data a brokerage produces
and never keeps is demand signal, and the place it escapes is the gap between a
public website and whatever happens to an enquiry after it arrives. So the site
and the follow-up behind it were built as one thing rather than a site handed
over with a contact form bolted on.

What they own as a result. Every enquiry is a record: what someone
searched for, what they asked about, what they did next. That accumulates on
their side rather than inside a portal that rents it back to them. A brokerage
that has been capturing its own demand signal for two years is not in the same
position as one that has not.
